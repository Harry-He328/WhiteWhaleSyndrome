## 方案一 帆绳控制航行

+Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished, readable 3D game environment concept image for a first-person room puzzle game. Landscape 1536x1024 composition. Camera is the player's own eyes while standing close to an ordinary waist-high wooden table and looking DOWN at about 45 degrees. We must clearly see the entire near table edge, controls within hand reach, the miniature sea on top, and some enclosing room walls beyond. NOT a top-down orthographic shot, NOT a full-size ship deck, NOT a third-person image. The tabletop itself contains a contained rectangular miniature living ocean with small waves and ONE small ancient wooden sailing ship visibly sailing, sails filled and a clear wake. The tabletop ocean and its tiny sailing ship are the main central subject. Human scale controls belong to the surrounding ROOM and near table edge, not to the tiny ship. An uncluttered enclosed nautical room, warm wood, readable blue water, soft daylight, evocative yet practical game concept art. No HUD, no menus, no decorative clutter or extra stations.
COMMON PLAYER INPUTS: a compact wooden ship steering wheel, about forearm-span diameter, mounted centrally to an angled bracket on the player's side of the table BELOW the sea's sightline; it controls the tiny sailing ship. On the near right table corner, a compact short bronze cannon on a wooden carriage, visibly recognizable as a cannon, pointing away from player toward the miniature ocean. Its rear breech faces the player. A short firing lanyard physically attaches to a clearly visible breech firing mechanism and ends in a large graspable wooden ring, conveniently beside the carriage. Show this as a coherent small weapon mechanism, not an arbitrary rope hanging from the steering wheel. This room-side cannon is the firing controller linked to the tiny ship's guns. All controls fit along the existing table edges; no separate floor furniture.
Use just three elegant small Chinese annotation labels outside the main sea area, with fine leader lines terminating precisely on the wheel, the travel controller, and the firing ring. Legible simple typography, no lengthy paragraphs. Exact wheel label: “船舵：控制转向”. Exact firing label: “拉环：手动开火”. Readability and believable positions matter more than ornament. VARIANT A, exact small title “方案一 帆绳控制航行”. Add a small but unmistakably ROOM-SCALE functional canvas sail on a short wooden mast clamped at the near LEFT table corner, outside the miniature ocean, to the player's left. This sail is separate from the tiny ship's sails: roughly chair-back sized, only modestly higher than the tabletop, placed off to the side so it does not obscure the ocean or ship. Show a compact yard and visible rope routing down from sail through a pulley to a readily reachable wooden cleat / hauling handle at the left near edge. The input focal point is this sail rope, and its physical connection to the room sail must be visually obvious. Sail currently deployed and the tiny ship sailing. Exact third label “帆绳：扬帆／收帆”, with leader ending at the reachable rope handle. Do not include a player-side anchor in this variant. Three control focal points: sail rope on left, wheel center, cannon firing ring right. All are spatially clear from first-person view.
